How does it work?

The board is the arena, the squishies are the players — and the phone says when to grab, who to trade with, and when everything goes BOOM.

  1. Lay out the board

    Two players, one on each side. Each side has hand markers and colorful circles.

  2. Pick a game in the app

    5 games — each with its own rules, sounds and timers.

  3. Listen to the phone

    The app counts down, spins, plays the signal and keeps score. No arguments!

  4. Who wins?

    A celebration on screen — and another round.
