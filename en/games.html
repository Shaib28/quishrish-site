The Games

5 games for two players. In every game the app leads the way: it counts down, spins, plays the signal and keeps score.

Quish Snap!

SpeedFocus

Who'll be first to grab the squishy from the jackpot? Only when the sound plays!

How to play

  1. Choose a game type: for points (pick how many points to win) or for squishies — the winner takes the squishy.
  2. Put a squishy on the jackpot in the middle of the board, and each player places their hands on the markers on their side.
  3. Tap "Ready!" — a countdown, then a ticking clock. The wait is random, so there's no guessing.
  4. When the SNAP sounds — grab! First to grab gets a point.
  5. Lift your hand before the sound and your opponent gets the point.

Pro Mode

Adds tricky decoy sounds during the wait. Only grab on the real SNAP!

Trade

SwapsFair play

Swap squishies with no arguments — the app draws who swaps with whom.

How to play

  1. Choose how many squishies each player offers (1–5) and place them on the marked circles.
  2. Tap "Trade!" — the app shows one swap at a time: two circles blink and the squishies fly to each other's spot.
  3. Swap them on the board and tap "Swapped!" — until all swaps are done.

Live Trade

For free-form deals: put the phone between the players and tap ✓ deal, ✕ no, or + add more.

Quish Order

MemorySpeed

Look closely — you only get 5 seconds!

How to play

  1. Choose how many points to play to, and the wheel picks who solves first.
  2. The solver turns around. The other player arranges squishies on the circles and snaps a photo of the row.
  3. The solver looks at the arrangement for just 5 seconds, then turns around again.
  4. The other player shuffles them — and the solver has 15 seconds to restore the right order.
  5. Compare with the photo: right order — a point! Then switch roles.

Quish Boom!

NerveReflexes

The fuse is burning… who'll be holding the squishy when the bomb explodes?

How to play

  1. Choose what to do before each pass: one squeeze, a bounce, or a stop on the jackpot.
  2. Choose how many explosions until you lose, and tap "Start timer".
  3. Pass the squishy back and forth while the fuse burns. The explosion time is random!
  4. BOOM! — tap the player who was holding the squishy, and their opponent gets a point.

Quish Target

AimMath

Throw squishies at the target and count down your points — to exactly 0!

How to play

  1. Choose your starting points (for example 200), and the wheel picks who goes first.
  2. Lay the phone sideways on the table. On each turn, throw a squishy at the target.
  3. Tap where it landed in the app: 10, 20, 50 or Jackpot 100 — and the points go down. Outside the target = a miss.
  4. You must reach exactly 0. A hit that would take you below zero doesn't count.
