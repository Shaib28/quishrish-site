Privacy Policy — QUISHRISH

Last updated: October 22, 2026

QUISHRISH is a companion app for the QUISHRISH board game. It is made for families and children and is designed not to collect any personal information.

The app is developed and published by ANIV TOYS (GYRO 26 INTERNATIONAL LTD), Israel.

What information do we collect?

None. The app does not send data to any server, does not use analytics or tracking tools, does not show ads, and does not require sign-up or an account. It works without an internet connection.

Camera and photos

  • The camera is used to photograph the squishy arrangement in "Quish Order" and to photograph squishies for the personal collection.
  • Collection photos, names and favorites are stored only on the device, in the app's own folder. They are never uploaded and no one but the user can see them.
  • Photo library access is used only when the user chooses to pick an existing photo for the collection or to save a squishy photo to the gallery.
  • Deleting the app deletes all of its photos and data from the device.

Settings

The app stores simple preferences on the device (for example, whether sound is muted). This information never leaves the device.

Children

Because the app collects no information, it collects no information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with its date.
