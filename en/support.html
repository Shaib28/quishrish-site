FAQ & Support

Got a question? The answer is probably here. If not — write to us.

What do I need to play?

The QUISHRISH game board, squishies, and two players. Download the app for free — it's the referee, the timer and the buzzer.

Does the app cost anything?

No. The app is free, with no ads and no in-app purchases.

What language is the app in?

The app interface is currently in Hebrew. Most instructions are shown with pictures, sounds and animations, so the games are easy to follow.

What ages is it for?

The games suit kids who can take turns and follow simple instructions — and the whole family. No reading needed for most of the play.

I can't hear any sounds. What should I do?

Check that your phone's volume is up and that sound isn't turned off on the app's main menu (the speaker icon at the bottom). The games play sound even when the iPhone's silent switch is on.

The screen turned off mid-game?

The app keeps the screen on during play. If it still turns off, check that Low Power Mode isn't on.

Where are "My Collection" photos stored?

Only on your device, inside the app. They're never uploaded to any server. To back up a squishy, tap it in your collection and choose "Save to gallery".

I deleted the app. Is my collection gone?

Yes — deleting the app also deletes the photos inside it. Squishies you saved to your gallery stay there. A regular device backup (such as iCloud) also keeps the app's data.

Why does the app ask for camera access?

The camera is used to photograph the arrangement in "Quish Order" and to photograph squishies for your collection. You can play without allowing it — just compare by eye.
